# Product Mission (Lite)

Tikel is a chat-based nutrition app that helps health-conscious individuals make informed eating decisions by providing AI-powered food scanning, personalized diet planning, and integrated health coaching through a conversational interface.

Tikel serves busy professionals and health-focused individuals who need convenient nutrition tracking and meal planning assistance. Unlike traditional nutrition apps with complex interfaces, Tikel provides all functionality through natural chat interactions with AI-powered scanning and real-time restaurant guidance that connects to personalized diet plans.
