## 2025-09-24: Initial Product Planning

**ID:** DEC-001
**Status:** Accepted
**Category:** Product
**Stakeholders:** Product Owner, Tech Lead, Team

### Decision

Tikel will be developed as a chat-based nutrition app focused on AI-powered food scanning, personalized diet planning, and integrated health coaching. The target market is health-conscious individuals and busy professionals seeking convenient nutrition tracking through conversational interfaces.

### Context

The nutrition app market is saturated with complex interfaces that require manual data entry, creating barriers for consistent usage. There's an opportunity to differentiate through conversational AI, comprehensive food intelligence, and integrated restaurant guidance that connects to personal diet plans.

### Alternatives Considered

1. **Traditional nutrition tracking app with manual entry**
   - Pros: Proven market model, simpler initial development
   - Cons: High user abandonment, requires extensive manual effort, poor restaurant integration

2. **Subscription-first premium nutrition platform**
   - Pros: Higher revenue per user, advanced features from launch
   - Cons: Limited user acquisition, high barrier to entry, complex initial development

### Rationale

The conversational interface reduces friction for daily nutrition tracking while AI-powered scanning eliminates manual data entry barriers. The freemium model allows broad user acquisition while premium features provide advanced intelligence and personalization.

### Consequences

**Positive:**
- Lower user acquisition costs through intuitive chat interface
- Higher engagement through connected ecosystem of features
- Scalable premium revenue through advanced AI features
- Competitive differentiation in crowded nutrition app market

**Negative:**
- Complex AI/ML development requirements for accurate food recognition
- Higher initial development costs for conversational interface
- Dependency on third-party food databases and nutrition APIs
