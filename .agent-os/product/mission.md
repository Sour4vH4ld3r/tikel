# Product Mission

## Pitch

Tikel is a chat-based nutrition app that helps health-conscious individuals make informed eating decisions by providing AI-powered food scanning, personalized diet planning, and integrated health coaching through a conversational interface.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Health-conscious individuals**: People actively seeking to improve their nutrition and make better food choices
- **Diet planners and trackers**: Users who want comprehensive meal planning with calorie and nutritional guidance

### User Personas

**Health-focused Professional** (25-45 years old)
- **Role:** Busy working professional
- **Context:** Limited time for meal planning but committed to healthy eating
- **Pain Points:** Difficulty tracking nutrition accurately, making quick healthy food decisions, understanding ingredient impacts
- **Goals:** Maintain healthy weight, optimize nutrition, save time on meal planning

**Diet Management User** (30-55 years old)
- **Role:** Individual managing specific health conditions or weight goals
- **Context:** Need structured guidance for diabetes, weight loss, or muscle gain
- **Pain Points:** Lack of personalized diet advice, difficulty staying on track with meal plans, restaurant food uncertainty
- **Goals:** Achieve specific health targets, maintain consistent eating habits, balance restaurant meals with daily goals

## The Problem

### Nutrition Tracking Complexity

Traditional food tracking apps require manual entry and provide limited contextual guidance. Users struggle to understand ingredient impacts and make quick, informed decisions about food choices, leading to inconsistent nutrition tracking.

**Our Solution:** Tikel uses AI-powered scanning to instantly analyze any food with contextual health advice.

### Disconnected Meal Planning

Diet planning tools exist separately from food tracking, creating fragmented experiences where users can't easily connect their daily choices to their health goals, especially when eating out.

**Our Solution:** Tikel integrates meal scanning, diet planning, and restaurant guidance into one conversational experience.

### Limited Food Intelligence

Existing apps show calories but lack actionable intelligence about ingredients, portion balance, and personalized recommendations based on individual health goals.

**Our Solution:** Tikel provides premium ingredient analysis, storage advice, recipe suggestions, and meal rebalancing based on personal diet plans.

## Differentiators

### Conversational Food Intelligence

Unlike traditional nutrition apps with complex interfaces, Tikel provides all functionality through natural chat interactions. Users simply scan, ask, or type to get personalized nutrition guidance, making healthy choices feel like having a nutrition coach in your pocket.

### Integrated Restaurant Companion

Unlike standalone meal planners, Tikel seamlessly connects restaurant menu scanning with personal diet plans. Users get real-time advice on how restaurant choices fit their daily goals, with premium "rebalance" features to adjust the rest of their day automatically.

### Connected Food Ecosystem

Unlike isolated tracking apps, Tikel connects every food interaction - from pantry management to meal scanning to diet planning. This creates a complete food intelligence system where expiry alerts, recipe suggestions, and meal planning work together seamlessly.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Packaged Food Scan:** Photo or barcode scanning with instant calorie and harmful ingredient identification
- **Fresh Food Recognition:** AI identification of vegetables, fruits, and grains with nutritional data per 100g
- **Meal Analysis:** Full plate scanning with dish identification, portion estimation, and calorie breakdown
- **Restaurant Menu Intelligence:** Menu photo scanning with clickable item selection and calorie comparison

### Tracking Features

- **Smart Food Diary:** Automatic logging from all scans with weekly/monthly analytics and streak tracking
- **Pantry Management:** Grocery tracking with expiry alerts and recipe suggestions based on available ingredients
- **Personalized Diet Planning:** Goal-based meal plans for weight loss, diabetes, muscle gain, or balanced nutrition

### Premium Features

- **Advanced Ingredient Analysis:** Detailed explanations of what each ingredient means with consumption frequency advice
- **Recipe Integration:** Cooking suggestions based on pantry items and dietary preferences
- **Meal Rebalancing:** Automatic adjustment of remaining daily meals when restaurant choices exceed plan limits
- **Comprehensive Analytics:** Detailed charts, reports, and health trend analysis
